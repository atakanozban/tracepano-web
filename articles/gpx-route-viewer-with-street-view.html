Viewer Architecture

GPX Route Viewer with 360° Street View & Live Minimap

A flat line on a map rarely tells the whole story of a corridor, road inspection, or trail. Pairing .gpx tracks with synced 360° street-level panoramas gives mapping teams both top-down spatial context and ground-level visual clarity in a single browser window.

Why Top-Down GPX Lines Alone Leave Context Gaps

Standard GPX viewers plot coordinates over a base map, which is fine for basic mileage checks. Yet whenever operations teams need to confirm road conditions, signage, trail access, or surrounding terrain, a polyline alone forces them to switch between separate Street View windows and desktop software.

Combining the route path with street-level imagery inside one interface removes that context switch.

Core Capabilities of a Synced GPX & Street View Workspace

Interactive Map & Satellite Overlay

Tracepano renders your uploaded .gpx track over an interactive map with satellite overlay support. Operators can inspect how the captured route aligns with real-world roads, intersections, and physical terrain directly in the browser.

Full-Screen 360° Panoramas with Corner Minimap

Inside the viewer, full-screen 360° street-level panoramas stay synced with a live corner minimap. As you pan around or move along the route, the minimap updates your exact camera position and orientation so you never lose your bearings.

Fast Route Switching & Real-Time Search

Instead of reloading separate pages for each file, teams can switch between routes from the left panel and use real-time search to jump directly to a location by title or city. You can preview the workspace layout in our Features overview.

Total Tracked Mileage Metrics

Alongside visual inspection, Tracepano calculates total tracked mileage across your routes so field coordinators can verify coverage progress at a glance without exporting CSV summaries.

Accessible Across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile Browsers

Because the viewer runs in standard web browsers powered by your own Google Maps API key, field operators and office reviewers work from the same up-to-date archive on desktop, tablet, or mobile devices—with zero local GIS installation required.
