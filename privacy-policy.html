Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tracepano collects, uses, and protects information when you visit https://tracepano.com or submit a contact or demo request through our website.

1. Data Controller & Contact Information

Tracepano operates out of Pécs, Hungary. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, you can reach us at info@tracepano.com or via our Contact Us page.

2. Google Tag Manager & Google Analytics

Tracepano uses Google Tag Manager (container ID GTM-P4CNVDWJ) to load and manage measurement tags, including Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited.

Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve page performance and documentation. Depending on your browser settings, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to collect aggregated and pseudonymized information such as:

  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation flow
  • Referral source (how you arrived at the website)
  • Approximate geographic region (derived without storing precise street addresses)
  • Device type, operating system, screen resolution, and browser version

Google Tag Manager itself does not collect personal information; it facilitates the firing of tags that may collect usage data as described above. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google Search Console

We use Google Search Console to monitor how search engines index tracepano.com, verify technical crawl health, and view aggregated search query statistics (such as impressions and clicks). Google Search Console operates on aggregated search data provided by Google and does not place tracking cookies on visitors browsing our site.

4. Contact & Demo Request Form (Web3Forms)

When you submit an inquiry or demo request on contact.html, the form collects only the information you choose to enter: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Country, City, and Your Message.

Submissions are processed via Web3Forms and delivered to our team solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry, evaluating your workflow requirements, or scheduling a guided Tracepano demo. We do not sell or rent your contact details to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Contact and demo request details are retained only as long as necessary to follow up on your request or maintain an active business conversation, unless a longer period is required by applicable law. Aggregated analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics property retention settings.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or another jurisdiction with comparable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete details
  • Request deletion (erasure) or restriction of processing of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent or object to processing where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, please email info@tracepano.com.
