STREET VIEW & GPX MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Manage your spatial routes and 360° street-level captures in one clean workspace.

Organizing field captures and GPX tracks across multiple locations takes precision. Keeping track of routes, mileage, and street-level imagery shouldn't require bloated software or locked-in mapping platforms.

Tracepano is a lightweight spatial management platform that brings your GPX files and 360° street views together in a single browser-based dashboard—powered directly by your own Google Maps API key.

What does Tracepano do?

Built for mapping teams, field operators, and businesses, Tracepano lets you:

  • 1. Upload and organize GPX tracks aloangside 360° street-level captures in one structured gallery,
  • 2. Connect your own Google Maps API key to render maps and street views with zero platform lock-in,
  • 3. Inspect tracked paths, location histories, and total distance directly on an interactive map,
  • 4. Keep all spatial files centralized without managing heavy desktop GIS tools or complex servers,
  • 5. Access and review your entire route archive anytime from any modern web browser.

Scale your field mapping operations cleanly while keeping full ownership of your API usage and spatial data.
